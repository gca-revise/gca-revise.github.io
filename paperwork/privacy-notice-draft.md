# GCA Revise: privacy notice (DRAFT for the school's DPO to check)

*Status: draft written by Claude, not legal advice. Square brackets [like this] are things the school must fill in. Written for students aged 11 to 16, so it is short and plain. The school may want a longer version for parents.*

*This notice describes the version with school sign-in. Until sign-in is switched on, GCA Revise stores nothing about you anywhere except on your own device.*

---

## What is GCA Revise?
GCA Revise is a website that helps you learn quotes and key words for your English texts. It was made for [School name] by [teacher name / role].

## What information does it keep about me?
**Without signing in:** your progress (which words and quotes you have learned, your points and your rank) is saved on the device you are using. It never leaves that device. If the school computer is wiped, it is lost.

**When you sign in with your school Microsoft account:** the site asks Microsoft to confirm who you are. It then keeps:
- your school email address and name, as given by Microsoft (needed so that you can sign in)
- a private ID number for your account
- your progress: what you have learned, what you got wrong last time, your points and rank, and when you last used the site

It does **not** keep: your date of birth, home address, photos, your location, your marks or grades, or anything you type that is not an answer in a quiz. It has **no adverts**, **no tracking** and **no analytics**.

## Why does it keep this?
So your progress follows you from one computer to another, and so your teacher can help you revise. [The school to confirm the legal reason. Normally for a school this is "public task" (helping you learn), not consent.]

## Who can see it?
- You can see your own progress.
- [Your English teacher / the teachers the school names] can see [class progress, if this feature is built. Today there is no teacher view. Delete this line if there is none.].
- The person who runs the site, [name, role], can see it if needed to fix a problem.
- Our storage company, Supabase, holds the information for us. It is held in London. It may only use it to run the site for us, not for its own purposes.
- Nobody else. We never sell it, share it for adverts, or use it to make decisions about you.

## How long is it kept?
[School to confirm.] Suggested: until you leave the school, or until the end of the school year after you finish your GCSE, whichever is first. Accounts that have not been used for [12 months] are deleted.

## Your rights
You (or your parent) can ask [contact name and email] to:
- show you what is held about you
- correct it
- delete it (we will do this within [one month]; usually it takes a few days)
- stop using it

You can also reset your progress yourself on the site ("Reset my progress"), but that only clears the copy on your device [update once reset also clears the saved copy online].

If you are unhappy, tell [School DPO name and email]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

## Who is responsible for this information?
[School name] is the "controller": it decides why the information is used. The person who built GCA Revise looks after it on the school's behalf (the "processor") and follows the school's instructions. [School to confirm this arrangement.]

## If something goes wrong
If your information is lost or seen by someone who should not see it, we will tell the school the same day, and the school will decide who must be told, including you and the ICO where the law requires it.
